# G3-Merch: Bildvorbereitung

Erzeugt mit integriertem Imagegen. Neutrale Produktvorlagen ohne Logo; die unveränderte bestehende logo.png wurde anschließend in SVG-Mockups eingebettet. Acht horizontale Vorlagen ergeben 18 getrennte Farbansichten. Alle Darstellungen sind Gestaltungs-Mockups, keine Herstellerfotos.

## g3-college-jacke

Use case: product-mockup. Generate a clean premium ecommerce MOCKUP CONTACT STRIP of classic varsity college jacket, snap buttons, ribbed collar cuffs and hem, front view, no hanger. Exactly 2 equally wide square panels in ONE horizontal row, total aspect ratio 2:1. Every panel is exactly the same product, same scale, same camera, same placement, changing ONLY color: panel 1 forest green body with white sleeves; panel 2 charcoal body with white sleeves. Cream studio background #F9F5F0 continuous edge to edge; no visible panel borders or separators. Front-lit photorealistic catalog quality with gentle natural contact shadows and believable fabric or product texture. Products centered in each panel with 12% empty margin all around, fully visible. Each product fills about 75% of panel height. No people, hands, models, hangers, accessories or props. IMPORTANT all products completely BLANK: no logo, no text, no lettering, no patch, no tag graphics, no watermark. A real logo will be composited separately. Matched framing in all panels so each color can be cropped into a separate square product image.
