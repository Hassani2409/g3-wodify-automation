# G3 professional refinement

## Preserve
Original green/orange brand, bear logo, centered first-screen headline, homepage subject order (training benefits, programmes, box, coaches, getting started), Next.js routes, login/shop/cart/wishlist access, Python API contract. The separate previous premium concept is not the visual authority.

## Refine
Deep logo green #13401e; neutral paper #f7f8f4; orange #ff8a59 for the main action. Dark text on orange. Barlow Condensed 600/700 headings and Open Sans variable body, both self-hosted. Two type families. Shared 5.5vw desktop / 20px phone content margins. 3px action corners, genuine photographs, no decorative gradients, urgency badges, rating strips, icon-feature cards or fabricated quotes.

## Composition and motion
Centered introductory headline, wide team photograph with a narrow training crop; offset text/photo sections and unboxed programme descriptions below. The brand and layout preference take precedence over the randomly dealt editorial-report direction (seed 1026592505); use its wide photography and typographic hierarchy, not its unrelated stock imagery or asymmetric masthead.

Content stays visible by default. One subtle photo entrance plus one-time element motion, control feedback, reduced-motion support. Navigation is a normal sticky bar with a plain mobile disclosure.

## Public routes
Homepage, training, about, coaches, prices and contact share the system. Shop, portal, blog, schedules, legal pages and other legacy routes remain; their content and operations need separate production review. Header mega-menu presentation is simplified; equivalent destination links remain.

## Honest transactions
Old pricing used placeholder Stripe IDs and a fallback that could display success after failed lead submission. The refined price selector leads to the honest trial request. Stripe server endpoints and shop remain unchanged. No online membership purchase is represented as configured.
